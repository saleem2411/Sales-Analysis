# Sales Analysis Dashboard
# Project Objective
To develop an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard in Power BI that enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, analyze customer purchasing behavior, evaluate product category performance, and identify revenue trends. The dashboard aims to transform raw sales data into actionable insights through data modeling, visualization, and KPI tracking, supporting data-driven decision-making and business growth.
# Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/saleem2411/Sales-Analysis/blob/main/Sales%20Analysis%20DB.csv">Dataset</a>
# KPIS
- What is the total revenue generated?
- What is the average order value?
- How many total orders were placed?
- Who are the top 5 customers by revenue contribution?
- Which product category received the most orders?
- Which age groups generate the highest revenue?
- Which payment method generates the highest revenue?
- How has revenue changed over time (monthly/yearly)?
- Which gender generates the highest revenue?

# Process
- Gather raw sales data from Kaggle.com
- Performed data cleaning by removing duplicates, handling missing values, remove inconsistencies in Microsoft Excel by using Power Query.
- Defined key performance indicators (KPIs): Revenue, Orders,Average Order Value.
- Implemented interactive slicers for: Gender, Product Category.
- Designed and developed an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance across customer demographics, product categories, and payment methods.
- Applied data modeling techniques to optimize dashboard performance and reporting.

# Project Insights
- The business generated approximately 681M in total revenue from 750K orders, resulting in an Average Order Value (AOV) of 908.79.
- Female(**342M**) customers generated slightly higher revenue compared to Male(**339M**) customers.
- The top 5 customers collectively contributed a significant portion of total revenue, highlighting the importance of customer retention and loyalty programs.
- Customers in the above 40 age group contributed the highest revenue, indicating that this segment is the most valuable customer demographic.

# Final Conclusion
By tracking key metrics such as Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), the dashboard enables stakeholders to evaluate sales performance, understand customer behavior, identify top-performing segments, and monitor revenue trends over time. These insights support data-driven decision-making and help businesses optimize marketing strategies, improve customer engagement, and drive revenue growth.
